PHOSPHOR TOOLS  -  launcher for the Phosphor apps
================================================

No install needed.

1. Right-click the zip > "Extract All..." > Extract, and move the
   PhosphorTools folder somewhere of your own, like Documents or the
   Desktop. (Keep the folder together: PhosphorTools.exe needs the
   "_internal" folder next to it.)

2. Double-click PhosphorTools.exe. If Windows says "Windows protected
   your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".

A small circle appears. Click it and the apps fan out around it:

  Phosphor XP      EXP / hour overlay
  Phosphor Count   boss respawn timers
  PhosphorWatch    Twitch stream viewer

Click an app to install it (first time only), start it, hide it, or
show it again. Hiding doesn't close it. Drag the circle anywhere;
right-click it for colors, opacity, "Start with Windows" and more.

Apps are installed in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PhosphorTools\apps and keep
themselves updated. The launcher's settings are in
%APPDATA%\PhosphorTools.
